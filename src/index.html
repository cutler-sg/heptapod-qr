    How the marks work & what survives

    The source packs text, a mode bit and a CRC-16 into ordered decimal digits. Each ring slot has one of five swelling classes, with or without an outward tendril. Reading begins beside the gap and follows the ordered outer-path points clockwise. These are serialized geometry rules, not the film’s language.

    The source decoder ignores styles and transforms. It reads coordinates, not photographs. Every source variant is round-trip checked. The artist renderer keeps the ordered mark classes and mandatory tendrils but changes widths and polygon sampling; it has no supported decoder. Its decorative tendrils, spatter and terminal taper carry no additional text. Decorative mode uses hash-derived art only.
