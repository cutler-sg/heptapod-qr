# Tattoo-artist review checklist

- [ ] Confirm the intended payload, exact case and punctuation with Michael. Distinguish literal “Michael”, chosen “Who is like God?”, and decoration.
- [ ] Confirm that this is original Arrival-inspired fan work, not a canonical alien translation or film glyph.
- [ ] Compare the preserved source and artist derivative. Keep both files. Agree which visible elements may be edited; the derivative carries no supported decoding guarantee.
- [ ] Confirm final unmirrored orientation and placement. Use the separately labelled mirrored transfer only for the transfer process.
- [ ] Print at 100% / Actual size with fitting disabled. Measure the 50 mm scale bar with a physical ruler. Confirm canvas and actual ink bounds; these are different measurements.
- [ ] Review every structural width, negative-space gap, clipping/collision warning, small detached spatter mark and terminal taper tip at actual size.
- [ ] Inspect curved joins and channels manually; the geometric checker is sampled and is not a complete proof of minimum feature size. Remove optional ornament only through an agreed design change, and preserve the original source separately.
- [ ] Consider placement, curvature, movement, skin and your own technique before choosing minimum widths/gaps. No software number guarantees healing, safety or longevity.
- [ ] Simplify and redraw as you judge appropriate, label the final result an artist derivative, and make no claim that arbitrary edits, skin, aging or photographs remain machine-decodable.
- [ ] Decide whether reversed/negative-space work is desired. Reversal changes which regions are ink, rather than merely changing the preview background.
- [ ] Handle project and manifest files as private: they contain the selected text. The encoded source can reveal it even without metadata.
- [ ] Approve final dimensions, consented wording and final stencil together before tattooing.
